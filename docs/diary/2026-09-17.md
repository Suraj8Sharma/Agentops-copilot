# Day 22 — OAuth failure-aware connections

OAuth Connections now consumes the real connection list and renders provider-specific expired, revoked, and scope-mismatch states without optimistic updates. Expired and revoked connections retain a visible re-auth prompt, Google testing-mode expiry is flagged ahead of time, Gmail send and draft failures have distinct outcomes, and re-authentication reuses the existing provider connect endpoint. Provider cards remain independent, and a failed disconnect leaves the server-confirmed state visible.
