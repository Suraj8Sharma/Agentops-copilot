# Day 16 — Real approval decisions

The Approval Screen now loads the authenticated user's pending approval steps from `GET /approvals/pending`, renders the backend explanation text verbatim, and records explicit approve/reject decisions through `POST /approvals/{step_id}`. Loading, empty, authentication, stale-decision, network, and retry states are surfaced without auto-approving; controls lock during submission, and the backend logs and resumes the LangGraph checkpoint after a decision.
